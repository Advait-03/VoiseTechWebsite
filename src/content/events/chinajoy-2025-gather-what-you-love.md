The 22nd China Digital Entertainment Expo & Conference (ChinaJoy) will be held at the Shanghai New International Expo Center from August 1st to 4th, 2025. The new slogan of ChinaJoy 2025 is: _Gather What You Love!_

As one of the most well-known and influential industry annual events in the field of games and digital entertainment in the world, ChinaJoy focuses on gaming as its core business covering a wide range of digital entertainment fields, including comic & animation, Internet film, music, e-literature, e-sports, designer toys, smart entertainment software and hardware. The event attracts more than 300,000 industry professionals, game players, comic & animation fans, hardware geeks to attend every year.

ChinaJoy 2024 lasted for four days, with a total of 367,000 visitors. 11 exhibition halls were used, covering an area of 130,000 square meters among which the BTOC Interactive Entertainment Halls had a total of 9 halls with an exhibiting area of 110,000 square meters. 307 well-known domestic and foreign companies powerfully participated in the exhibition, engaging in games, IT, Internet, and consumer electronics industries.
