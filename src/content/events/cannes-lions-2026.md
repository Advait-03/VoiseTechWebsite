Five days in Cannes. Countless conversations, ideas, and perspectives that will stay with us long after the Croisette quiets down.

From **AI** and **retail media** to **CTV**, **data**, and **creative innovation**, one thing stood out throughout the week: creativity and technology are becoming more connected than ever, and the future of advertising will be built on both.

Cannes Lions once again brought together **brands, agencies, platforms, creators, and innovators** from across the globe to explore how creativity can drive business growth and cultural impact.

Beyond the stages and sessions, it was the conversations that mattered most. Reconnecting with industry peers, meeting new faces, exchanging ideas, and hearing different perspectives on where our industry is heading.

The week was a reminder that the best ideas rarely happen in isolation. They happen when great people, technology, and creativity come together in the same place.
