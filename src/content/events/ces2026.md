CES 2026 took place in Las Vegas from January 6–9 as a global technology conference and exhibition, bringing together leaders from technology, media, advertising, and consumer brands. The event featured **400+ conference sessions** with **1,300+ speakers** exploring how emerging technologies are transforming advertising, media, and consumer engagement.

Key voices included leaders from **AMD**, **Siemens**, and major global consultancies, alongside creative innovators and cultural figures such as **Serena Williams** and **will.i.am**, who shared perspectives on AI, creativity, and technology’s role in shaping modern consumer experiences.

The agenda covered topics including **AI-driven personalization**, **data privacy**, **connected TV**, **retail media**, immersive experiences, and the convergence of media, commerce, and technology. Dedicated tracks like CES Foundry, Digital Health, Mobility, and Eureka Park showcased platforms and solutions across advertising technology, data, automation, and next-generation brand storytelling, making CES 2026 a key forum for industry learning, partnerships, and innovation.
