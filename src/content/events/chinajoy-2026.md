VoiseTech attended **ChinaJoy 2026**, held from **31 July to 3 August** in **Shanghai**, one of the world's leading events for digital entertainment, gaming, AI, and technology innovation. The event brought together global brands, publishers, developers, technology providers, and digital ecosystem leaders shaping the future of interactive experiences.

The event featured discussions around AI, gaming, digital media, programmatic advertising, audience engagement, and emerging technologies, creating valuable opportunities for collaboration and industry networking. Our team engaged with brands, publishers, agencies, and technology partners to explore new opportunities and showcase how our omnichannel AI-powered AdTech infrastructure is enabling smarter, more transparent, and performance-driven programmatic and omnichannel advertising.
