The 2025 **Google APAC TV Summit** brought together top players from the connected TV space, including operators, device makers, platform developers, and Google partners. Held in Bangkok, the event explored advancements in Android TV, AI-driven personalisation, user experience design, and the future of big-screen entertainment in the APAC region. Attendees benefited from product showcases, expert-led sessions, and collaboration opportunities that highlighted the future of cloud-edge integration, device innovation, and sustainable growth across the TV ecosystem.
