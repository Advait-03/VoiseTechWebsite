## Objective

Drive stronger engagement and interactions across Southern markets through a multi-format digital campaign highlighting Hyundai’s innovative brand appeal.

## Our Approach

Voise Tech executed a programmatic banner campaign across premium digital environments using its proprietary adtech stack. The strategy focused on delivering precise audience targeting and creative variation across Bangalore, Chennai, and the rest of South India. By leveraging AI-led optimization and real-time performance data, Hyundai achieved maximum visibility among high-intent consumers across devices.

## Highlights

-   Hyundai drove higher engagement & interaction with multi-format campaigns across the Southern markets.
-   Targeting: Bangalore, Chennai & Rest of South India
-   3X higher engagement vs. benchmark
-   2X more precise targeting through AI-led optimization

## Metric Results

The campaign achieved 2,5 Mn impressions with a 0.23% CTR, delivering strong engagement and visibility across key southern markets.

## Impact

-   The campaign drove strong regional traction and consistent performance across formats and devices.
-   It showcased how programmatic precision, backed by creative agility, can elevate engagement and deliver meaningful brand outcomes for Hyundai.
