## Objective

Apple set out to build excitement for the Apple 17 through a high-impact video campaign targeting premium, tech-savvy audiences worldwide. The aim was to deliver quality attention and meaningful engagement in brand-safe, contextually relevant environments, creating an experience that felt distinctly Apple.

## Our Approach

Using Voise DSP’s precision targeting and curated global inventory, we delivered Apple 17 video creatives across premium, brand-safe placements. Contextual intelligence ensured relevance, from tech enthusiasts to lifestyle explorers, while real-time optimisation and human checkpoints elevated performance, driving a seamless and high-quality viewing experience at scale.

## Highlights

-   Delivered Apple’s video creatives across premium, brand-safe global publishers
-   Leveraged Voise Curated Lists to reach tech-first, lifestyle-driven audiences
-   Maintained consistently high viewability through transparent inventory selection
-   Real-time human + machine optimisation ensured contextual relevance

## Impact

Apple 17’s campaign strengthened brand visibility, delivered meaningful engagement with a strong CTR for a video-led push, achieved exceptional viewability, and ensured transparent, high-quality delivery across markets.
