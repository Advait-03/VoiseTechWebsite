## Objective

Mahindra aimed to enhance brand visibility and engagement across key southern markets by leveraging high-impact banner and video creatives. The goal was to connect with automotive enthusiasts and potential buyers through dynamic storytelling and data-led precision targeting.

## Our Approach

Voise Tech executed a cross-format campaign combining banner and video ads to deliver a cohesive brand experience. Using advanced audience segmentation and contextual intelligence, we targeted users across Bangalore, Chennai, and the rest of South India. Real-time optimisation ensured efficient delivery, higher engagement, and precise audience reach throughout the campaign.

## Highlights

-   Dynamic banner and video execution for enhanced storytelling.
-   Targeted premium audiences across key South Indian markets.
-   Real-time optimisation driving 3X higher engagement.
-   Achieved 2X more precise audience targeting with data-led delivery.

## Metric Results

The campaign achieved strong regional traction with consistent performance across devices, proving how programmatic precision and creative dynamism can elevate engagement and drive measurable brand outcomes.

## Impact

-   Strengthened Mahindra’s digital footprint in regional markets.
-   Delivered 3X higher engagement through multi-format creatives.
-   Boosted visibility and brand recall among automotive audiences.
-   Validated the power of creative + data synergy in performance uplift.
