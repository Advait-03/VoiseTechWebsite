## Objective

Etihad Airways aimed to inspire premium travellers by showcasing its world-class experiences and global network. The campaign focused on boosting visibility and engagement among travel, lifestyle, and finance audiences.

## Our Approach

A precision-led banner campaign powered by contextual targeting and Voise-curated premium inventory. By reaching high-value audiences across relevant moments, we maximised impact and reinforced Etihad’s luxury positioning.

## Highlights

-   Strong brand visibility across premium travel and lifestyle environments.
-   Curated audience segments ensured high engagement and contextual relevance.
-   Real-time optimization improved efficiency and click-through performance.
-   Campaign reinforced Etihad’s image as a luxury, experience-first airline.

## Metric Results

VoiseTech enabled Etihad Airways to engage travel-savvy audiences with contextual precision, achieving 1.3M impressions and 0.32% CTR.

## Impact

-   Strengthened Etihad’s digital presence among aspirational travellers.
-   Drove meaningful engagement through precision targeting and creative delivery.
-   Delivered consistent visibility across high-quality, brand-safe platforms.
-   Showcased the power of programmatic precision in driving global brand affinity.
