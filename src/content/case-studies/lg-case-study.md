## Objective

LG wanted to strengthen its brand visibility among a digitally active, tech-curious audience, driving awareness and engagement through dynamic banner creatives. The goal was simple: deliver impactful reach while keeping engagement quality high across premium, brand-safe environments.

## Our Approach

At **VoiseTech**, we leveraged our advanced programmatic platform and proprietary audience curation to connect LG with the right users, at the right time, on the right screens.

By combining lifestyle and technology-driven audience signals with our curated lists, we ensured every impression contributed to stronger recall and relevance.

## Highlights

-   Targeted 25–45-year-old men and women with an interest in lifestyle and technology.
-   Activated Voise Curated Lists for refined precision and reduced audience wastage.
-   Delivered LG’s message through high-impact banner formats across premium inventory.
-   Used data-led optimization to maximize performance and engagement efficiency.

## Metric Results

The campaign’s performance showcased how strategic audience segmentation and smart creative delivery can turn digital impressions into meaningful engagement, amplifying LG’s presence and driving awareness at scale.

## Impact

-   Doubled audience curation through premium placements.
-   Achieved precise reach within the female 25–45 segment.
-   Delivered strong global visibility and engagement.
-   Reinforced Voise Tech as a trusted global adtech partner.
