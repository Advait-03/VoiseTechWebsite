## Objective

ICICI Bank set out to boost awareness and engagement for its digital banking solutions among finance-savvy, news-focused audiences, connecting with consumers who value convenience, innovation, and trust.

## Our Approach

A multi-format campaign across Banner, Video, and CTV delivered a seamless brand story. Using curated audiences and contextual intelligence, we reached 25–45-year-old Lifestyle and Tech enthusiasts, optimising in real time to drive impact across premium screens.

## Highlights

-   Strong brand visibility across premium financial and news ecosystems.
-   Contextual targeting ensured relevant, high-intent audience engagement.
-   Smart optimization improved efficiency throughout the campaign lifecycle.
-   Seamless execution aligned with ICICI’s trusted and innovative brand positioning.

## Metric Results

ICICI Bank engaged finance and news audiences through VoiseTech’s smart targeting and storytelling, boosting brand impact and recall.

## Impact

-   Strengthened ICICI Bank’s digital footprint in competitive finance markets.
-   Achieved high visibility and deep engagement across screens.
-   Reinforced brand trust through contextual precision and consistent creative delivery.
-   Demonstrated how strategic data-led execution can drive quality awareness at scale.
