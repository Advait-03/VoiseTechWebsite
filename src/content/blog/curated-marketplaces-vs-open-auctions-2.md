Here’s an inconvenient truth: The programmatic strategy that dominated the last decade is quietly costing you money. Remember when open auctions were supposed to be the answer? Unlimited scale, bargain-basement CPMs, and programmatic paradise. Well, we got the scale. We also got bot traffic, sketchy Made-for-Advertising sites, and a brand safety nightmare.

Now, curated marketplaces are flipping the script.

But is the higher price tag worth it? Let’s cut through the noise.

## The Numbers Don’t Lie

Here’s what’s actually happening in the market right now:

In 2026, advertisers are spending more than twice as much on curated deals and private marketplaces (PMPs) compared to open exchanges. The reason? They’re tired of playing inventory roulette. Programmatic still accounts for the bulk of digital transactions globally, and sources show evolving supply path preferences.

But here’s the plot twist: Those curated marketplaces with the 40% higher CPMs? They’re delivering 25% better ROI.

Higher costs. Better returns. How does that math work?

## Open Auctions: The Wild West Still Exists

Open auctions promised scale and efficiency. And they delivered along with:

-   **Made-for-Advertising (MFA) sites:** Roughly $701 million, representing 10% of global open programmatic ad spend on websites, went to MFA sites in [Q1 of 2025 alone](https://www.pixalate.com/blog/q1-2025-global-made-for-advertising-mfa-benchmarks-reports-for-ctv-mobile-apps-and-websites).
-   **Bot traffic explosion:** Bot traffic now [accounts for 37%](https://www.imperva.com/resources/resource-library/reports/2025-bad-bot-report/) of all internet traffic. For the first time in a decade, automated traffic has overtaken human traffic.
-   **Hidden fees everywhere:** Only 36 cents of every programmatic dollar actually reaches publishers, with an [estimated $26.8 billion](https://www.ana.net/miccontent/show/id/rr-2023-12-ana-programmatic-media-supply-chain-transparency-study) lost to inefficiencies in 2025.

So yes, open auctions give you reach. But at what actual cost?

**Curated Marketplaces: More Than Just Expensive PMPs**

Here’s where it gets interesting. Curated marketplaces aren’t just “premium” open auctions. They’re fundamentally different:

-   **Pre-filtered quality:** Curated marketplaces pre-filter low-quality, non-viewable, and MFA (Made-for-Advertising) inventory, ensuring ads reach real users in brand-safe environments.
-   **Better match rates:** Curated Private Marketplaces (PMPs) enable more precise audience matching by activating first-party data in privacy-compliant ways.
-   **Real performance gains:** Brands leveraging curated inventory report stronger brand lift, higher purchase intent, and more predictable performance outcomes than standard open exchange buys.
