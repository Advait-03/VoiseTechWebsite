In a world where users seamlessly shift from mobile to desktop to smart TV, **cross-device targeting** has become a non-negotiable in programmatic advertising—especially in Q2 when strategic clarity can directly impact ROI. It’s no longer enough to reach your audience once; you need to **reach them consistently, contextually, and effectively** across their digital journey.

🚀 Why Cross-Device Targeting Matters

With the average consumer using **3 to 5 devices daily**, fragmented messaging leads to poor recall and wasted spend. Cross-device strategies not only improve **reach and frequency** but also help drive stronger **brand engagement and conversion accuracy.**

🔑 Best Practices for Q2 Success

-   **Leverage First-Party Data**Combine CRM data with authenticated user signals to build unified IDs and accurate audience profiles.
-   **Utilise Identity Graphs**Partner with platforms that offer advanced identity resolution to track users anonymously across screens.
-   **Ensure Creative Consistency**Develop creatives that are **adaptable** yet **cohesive** across mobile, desktop, CTV and more.
-   **Implement Sequential Messaging**Guide users through a funnel by using storytelling that evolves as they move across devices.
-   **Adopt Cross-Channel Attribution Models**Use attribution platforms that analyse touchpoints holistically—giving credit where it’s due and helping optimise ad spend.

📊 Key Metrics to Track

-   **Cross-device reach and frequency:** Understand how often your audience sees your ads across different devices to optimise exposure without over-saturation.
-   **Engagement rate per device:** Track how users interact with your ads on each device type to identify where engagement is highest and tailor creatives accordingly.
-   **Conversion lift by device combination:** Measure the increase in conversions when users are exposed to ads across multiple devices versus a single one.
-   **Path-to-conversion journey insights:** Gain visibility into the sequence of device interactions that lead to conversion, helping refine messaging at every touchpoint.
